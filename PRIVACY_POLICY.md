# Privacy Policy for ShiftSnap

**Effective Date:** October 14, 2026  
**Last Updated:** October 14, 2026

## 1. Introduction

Welcome to ShiftSnap — Staff Scheduling & Roster Planner (“ShiftSnap,” “we,” “our,” or “the app”).

ShiftSnap is a staff scheduling and workforce planning application that helps users manage employee information, create weekly work schedules, calculate working hours, estimate payroll, and export roster reports.

We respect your privacy and are committed to being transparent about how information is handled when you use ShiftSnap.

This Privacy Policy explains what information may be stored, how it is used, how it is protected, and what choices you have regarding your information.

ShiftSnap is designed to operate offline and store scheduling information locally on your device. Under the current design, the app does not require an account or send employee records to a ShiftSnap-operated server.

## 2. Information We Handle

### A. Employee Information

When you use the employee management features, you may enter:

- Employee names.
- Job titles, roles, or departments.
- Optional phone numbers.
- Hourly wage rates.
- Employee status, such as active or inactive.

This information is provided voluntarily by the user and is used to manage employee records within the app.

### B. Shift and Scheduling Information

You may enter:

- Shift dates.
- Start and end times.
- Break durations.
- Employee shift assignments.
- Job roles and departments.
- Optional shift notes.
- Scheduled working hours.

This information is used to organize work schedules, identify potential overlapping shifts, and calculate scheduled working hours.

### C. Payroll and Calculation Information

ShiftSnap may use hourly wage rates, working hours, break durations, and optional overtime settings to calculate estimated payroll.

These calculations are performed for scheduling and planning purposes. ShiftSnap is not a payroll processing service and does not automatically submit payments or tax filings.

### D. App Preferences

The app may store settings such as:

- Business or organization name.
- Preferred currency.
- Theme preference.
- First day of the week.
- Default shift duration and break duration.
- Overtime calculation preferences.
- Export and other app settings.

### E. Information We Do Not Intentionally Collect

Under the current design, ShiftSnap does not require users to provide an email address, create an account, or submit payment information.

The app is not designed to collect precise location, contacts, call logs, SMS messages, camera recordings, or microphone recordings.

ShiftSnap does not intentionally collect employee records for advertising or profiling purposes.

These statements describe the intended app design and must be updated if future versions introduce additional collection or processing.

## 3. How Information Is Stored

ShiftSnap is designed to store the information you enter locally on your device using browser storage, such as LocalStorage, or another local storage mechanism implemented in the app.

If ShiftSnap is installed as an Android application through Capacitor, information will be stored according to the storage technology used by that version.

Under the intended offline-first design:

- Employee records remain on your device.
- Shift schedules remain on your device.
- Wage rates and calculation settings remain on your device.
- App preferences remain on your device.
- No ShiftSnap account or cloud synchronization is required.

Local storage is not the same as encrypted storage. Anyone who gains access to your unlocked device or its app data may potentially access information stored there.

You are responsible for securing your device and limiting access to sensitive employee and wage information.

## 4. How We Use Information

Information entered into ShiftSnap is used only to provide the app's functionality, including:

- Creating and managing employee records.
- Creating and organizing weekly rosters.
- Assigning employees to shifts.
- Calculating scheduled working hours.
- Identifying overlapping shift assignments.
- Estimating payroll costs.
- Generating roster images and PDF reports.
- Saving your preferences.
- Creating and restoring data backups, when those features are available.

We do not intend to sell personal information or use employee records for targeted advertising.

## 5. Data Sharing and Third-Party Services

ShiftSnap is designed not to transmit employee records to ShiftSnap-operated servers.

We do not intentionally sell, rent, or share locally stored employee information with third parties for their own marketing purposes.

However, certain features may involve third-party software or services:

**Exporting and sharing files:** If you choose to share a roster image, PDF, or backup, the file may be sent to the recipient or service you select. The privacy practices of that recipient or service will apply after the file is shared.

**Hosting and app distribution:** Downloading the web app, installing updates, or obtaining ShiftSnap through an app store may involve the relevant hosting provider, browser, or platform processing technical information under its own policies.

**Third-party libraries:** ShiftSnap may use software libraries to provide features such as image export and PDF generation. Their actual behavior depends on the versions and configuration included in the published app.

If future versions add analytics, advertising, cloud synchronization, crash reporting, or other external services, this policy will be updated to explain the relevant data practices.

## 6. Exported Files and Backups

ShiftSnap may allow you to export rosters as PNG images or PDF documents and back up app data in JSON format.

These files may contain employee names, shift assignments, working hours, wage rates, or other information included in the export or backup.

You control where you save these files and whether you share them.

Please take appropriate precautions when exporting or sharing files containing employee or payroll information. Anyone with access to an unprotected exported file may be able to read its contents.

If you import a backup, the imported information may be added to or replace existing data depending on the app's implemented import process. Review the confirmation message before proceeding.

## 7. Data Retention and Deletion

ShiftSnap is designed to retain information locally until you delete it, clear the relevant app data, uninstall the app, or remove the information through an available in-app feature.

You can manage your locally stored information by using available editing and deletion features.

You may also clear the app's stored data through your device or browser settings. Doing so may permanently remove employee records, schedules, settings, and backups that have not been saved elsewhere.

Uninstalling the app or clearing its data may not remove files that you previously exported to a separate folder or shared with another application. Those files must be deleted separately.

Because the intended app design does not require a ShiftSnap account or maintain a ShiftSnap cloud database of employee records, there is no ShiftSnap account to delete under the current design.

If a future version introduces accounts or cloud storage, appropriate account and data deletion procedures will be described here.

## 8. Data Security

We aim to minimize data exposure by designing ShiftSnap to work offline and avoid unnecessary transmission of employee information.

You can help protect your data by:

- Using a screen lock on your device.
- Keeping your operating system and app updated.
- Restricting access to your device.
- Avoiding the sharing of unprotected roster exports.
- Keeping backup files in a secure location.
- Reviewing files before sharing them with other people or services.

No method of electronic storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure. We therefore cannot guarantee absolute security of locally stored information or exported files.

## 9. Permissions

ShiftSnap is intended to request only the permissions necessary for its implemented features.

The core scheduling functionality does not require access to your camera, microphone, contacts, SMS messages, phone calls, or precise location.

Saving or sharing exports may use browser download capabilities or Android system file-sharing and file-selection features, depending on the installed version.

Actual permissions may differ depending on the libraries, Capacitor plugins, and Android functionality included in a particular release.

## 10. Children's Privacy

ShiftSnap is designed as a general-purpose scheduling and workforce management tool, not specifically for children.

The app does not intentionally collect personal information from children through a ShiftSnap-operated online account or server under the intended offline-first design.

If you believe that a future version of ShiftSnap has collected information inappropriately, please contact us using the details provided below.

## 11. Your Responsibilities When Managing Employee Information

If you use ShiftSnap to store information about employees or other individuals, you are responsible for ensuring that you have an appropriate reason and any necessary authorization to enter and use that information.

Please avoid entering sensitive personal information that is not necessary for scheduling.

You are also responsible for handling exported reports and backups in accordance with applicable privacy, employment, and data protection requirements.
